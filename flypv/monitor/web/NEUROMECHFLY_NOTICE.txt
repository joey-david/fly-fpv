NeuroMechFly v2 geometry
========================

The live monitor loads simplified NeuroMechFly v2 mesh geometry at runtime from
flyplotlib's pinned mirror:

  https://github.com/tkclam/flyplotlib
  ref: 6801e03af8f6422524ff60d4c1c2b9a134f730d6
  path: src/flyplotlib/data/neuromechfly/

Those meshes and their rigging metadata originate from FlyGym / NeuroMechFly v2:

  https://github.com/NeLy-EPFL/flygym
  Wang-Chen et al. (2024), NeuroMechFly v2, Nature Methods
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02497-y

FlyGym and the NeuroMechFly assets are distributed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0. flyplotlib's accompanying NOTICE identifies its vendored assets as
FlyGym-derived and subject to the same license and attribution requirements.

flypv does not redistribute the STL files: the browser fetches the pinned source
assets and reduces them to display point clouds in memory. The wing planform is
then reposed using flypv's simulated stroke, deviation, angle of attack, and
stroke-plane tilt rather than using a canned animation.
